# RAG Retrieval Test Questions

This document contains categorized test questions for evaluating a legal RAG retrieval pipeline.

## Level 1: Direct Fact Retrieval
- What documents are required for a Lease Deed?
- Is registration mandatory for a lease agreement exceeding 11 months?
- What documents are required for a Deed of Adoption?
- What is the purpose of a Non-Disclosure Agreement?
- What are the required clauses in a Loan Agreement with Security?
- What is the limitation period for recovery of money?
- Under which section is anticipatory bail filed?
- What documents are needed for a legal notice for property partition?

## Level 2: Clause-Based Retrieval
- What happens if rent is not paid on time in a Lease Deed?
- Can the lessee sublet the leased property?
- What rights does a landlord have regarding inspection of the property?
- What remedies are available when an NDA is breached?
- What security is provided in the Loan Agreement template?
- Can the lender sell pledged shares if the borrower defaults?

## Level 3: Procedural Questions
- What is the procedure for executing a Lease Deed?
- How is a Family Trust created?
- What is the process for filing a legal notice for recovery of money?
- How do you execute a Deed of Adoption?
- What steps are involved in registering a family settlement deed?
- What is the procedure for obtaining anticipatory bail?

## Level 4: Comparative Questions
- What is the difference between a lease agreement and a rent agreement?
- How is a Family Trust different from a Family Settlement Deed?
- Compare a Legal Notice for Property Partition and a Legal Notice for Recovery of Money.
- What is the difference between an NDA and a Business Services Agreement?

## Level 5: Semantic Search Tests
- What paperwork should the landlord and tenant verify before signing?
- What proof of ownership must be checked before renting property?
- How can confidential business information be legally protected?
- What can a lender do if collateral loses value?
- How can family members divide inherited property without going to court?
- What options does a person have before filing a money recovery lawsuit?

## Level 6: Jurify-Style User Queries
- My tenant has not paid rent for 3 months. What does the lease template say?
- I want to rent out my property for 2 years. Do I need registration?
- My friend borrowed money and is refusing to return it. What should I do first?
- A builder has delayed possession of my apartment. What legal steps can I take?
- I want to protect my startup idea before discussing it with another company.
- My siblings and I want to divide inherited property amicably.

## Gold Standard Query Set
1. What is the difference between a lease agreement and a rent agreement?
2. Is registration mandatory for a lease exceeding 11 months?
3. What documents are required for a Deed of Adoption?
4. What clauses should an NDA contain?
5. What happens if rent is not paid on time?
6. What is the procedure for filing a legal notice for recovery of money?
7. What security is provided in the Loan Agreement template?
8. How can inherited property be divided without litigation?
9. Under which section is anticipatory bail filed?
10. What legal remedies exist for delay in possession by a builder?
